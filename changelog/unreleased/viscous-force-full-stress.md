* fixed: `viscous_force` and `wall_shear_stress` integrate the boundary viscous stress the momentum equation applies, `-ρ ν_eff (∇U + ∇Uᵀ - ⅔ tr(∇U) I)⋅n` with the boundary-face velocity gradient, instead of its tangential normal-gradient part only. The transposed and normal stress terms now enter the reported forces, which therefore change; zero-gradient patches report the force of their transposed stress, while `Slip` and `Symmetry` patches still report none.
